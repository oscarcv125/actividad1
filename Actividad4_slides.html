The data engineering pipeline

Raw data moves left to right. Every stage is a place where the data changes shape, owner, or guarantee.

When it becomes big data

Five properties, any of which can break a pipeline that worked fine at small scale.

Volume
How much there is to store and scan.
Velocity
How fast it arrives, and how fresh the answer has to be. A fraud check has a different budget than a monthly report.
Variety
Tables, logs, images, free text, all landing in the same lake.
Veracity
How much of it can be trusted: duplicates, drift, sensors that fail silently.
Value
Whether any of the above pays for the infrastructure it takes to hold it.

Four words that get used interchangeably

They describe different jobs, and in most companies different people.

Data engineering builds and operates the pipeline on the first slide. Its output is reliable, documented data that somebody else can use.

Data mining searches data that already exists for patterns worth knowing. It starts from the data and asks what is in here.

Data science starts from a business question, then answers it with statistics, models and judgement about what the answer is worth.

Machine learning is the family of algorithms that improve from examples instead of from written rules. Data science reaches for it when a question is predictive; the rest of the time a query or a regression table is enough.
